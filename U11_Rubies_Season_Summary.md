### Game 2 – vs Brisbane Knights | Win 6–0

**Summary:** A strong team performance resulting in a convincing 6–0 win. All players contributed well across the pitch.

**Key observations:**
- Sydney had an outstanding game, starting in midfield before moving to striker. Showed excellent determination, working through multiple tackles and converting efforts into goals.
- Naomi played in defence for the full match but kept roaming, leaving gaps. Worth trialling her in midfield where her natural movement can be an asset.
- Sienna delivered an excellent all-round performance, playing both GK and left wing. Made multiple strong saves in goal; strong distribution from the back.
- Charlotte appeared uncomfortable in midfield — worth trialling her back in defence.
- Strong high-pressing from opposition restarts — girls applied immediate pressure on opposition goal kicks and kickoffs, winning the ball back quickly.
- First half defensively solid; second half saw positional gaps — area to focus on.

**Things to work on:**
- Defensive positional discipline
- Naomi: ball control and getting kicks away
- Throw-ins — support players not near the thrower
- Corner kicks — same as throw-ins

### Game 5 – vs Toowong White | Win 3–1

**Goal scorers:** Taylor, Sydney, Pippa

**Summary:** A competitive and close game. Strong determination, resilience, and composure throughout. Probably the team's strongest defensive performance of the season — first game conceding a goal. Team spent large periods in the opposition half, controlling possession and territory.

**Player Notes:**

**Taylor** — Excellent game, one of the most dangerous players going forward. Won the ball deeper in the field and drove it forward. Frequently beat defenders on the outside and created chances. Key development area: decision-making after beating a defender — look to pass into space rather than take on another player.

**Sydney** — Solid, worked hard throughout. Contributed offensively and scored a goal. Reliable effort across the field.

**Pippa** — Strong game adapting to left wing (not her usual position). Scored a goal and worked hard both on and off the ball.

**Ruby** — Playing wing/left-sided attacker for the first time. Handled transition well. Natural instinct to drop back into defensive positions — work on staying higher when in possession.

**Charlotte** — Playing right defence but tended to roam left. May be more naturally suited to midfield where her movement creates value.

**Kiera** — Outstanding defensive performance. Consistently tracked runners, positioned well and won crucial defensive duels. Critical to the team's success.

**Nixie** — Very strong defensive game. Aggressive in getting to loose balls first, stepped forward to meet the ball early and clear danger. Excellent willingness to attack the ball.

**Poppy** — Strong defensive contribution. Important clearances and good commitment in defensive situations.

**Simone / Sienna (GK)** — Simone worked hard in goals. Sienna particularly impressive — real commitment and bravery, willingness to dive for the ball. Her GK performances gave the team confidence.

**Referee feedback:** Spoke to coaching staff at halftime regarding pushing and physical contact. Girls need to compete hard but keep elbows in and avoid leading with arms.

**Technical area:** Throw-in technique inconsistent — ball behind head, two hands, straight arm action, both feet on/behind the line.

**Key development theme:** Decision-making and distribution after beating a defender. Also: converting possession and territory into goals — we dominated the half but need better final-third combinations.

### Game 6 – vs Mitchelton Glitter Dragons | Win 1–0

**Goal scorer:** Taylor

**Summary:** One of the toughest and most hard-fought matches of the season. Despite spending most of the game in the opposition half, struggled to convert territory into goals. Strong wind and low sun made the first half difficult. Girls showed grit and determination to find a way to win despite not being at their fluent best.

**Player Notes:**

**Taylor** — Strong game on the right wing. Consistent ground down the flank, created several scoring opportunities, scored the winning goal. Occasionally found it hard when Mitchelton's left back tried to go around her — press hard but be prepared for her.

**Pippa** — Worked hard on the left wing. Occasionally found herself being shepherded towards the sideline and found it difficult to bring the ball back into dangerous central areas.

**Ruby** — Started strongly and showed she can hold her own further up the field. Put her hand up for more opportunities in attacking positions.

**Sydney** — Not quite at usual level but still contributed. Won the ball effectively but didn't always follow up with the second effort to apply more pressure.

**Mia** — Spent time in several positions and adapted well to each role. Another dependable performance. **Player of the Match.**

**Charlotte** — Solid game, contributed well whenever called upon.

**Naomi** — Usual reliable effort, recognised for strong team performance. **Player of the Match.**

**Poppy** — Outstanding match in defence. Aggressive in winning the ball, stepped into contests confidently and repeatedly cleared danger. **Player of the Match.**

**Kiera** — Rock solid at the back. Consistently tracked back to protect the goal, supported the GK and provided defensive cover whenever needed.

**Sienna** — Great composure in goal. Produced an excellent save when an attacker closed her down quickly. Adapted well to the technical demands.

**Coach's reflection:** We controlled large periods of play and spent most of the match in the attacking half, but didn't convert enough opportunities. Opposition teams often retreat almost all players into their own penalty area — we need to create better shooting lanes and follow up rebounds.

**Focus areas:**
- Converting territory into goals (shooting under pressure, following up rebounds)
- Creating space around the box (width, quick passes rather than dribbling into crowds)
- Second efforts and pressing after winning the ball

### Game 7 – vs Sapphires (2nd) | Draw 4–4

**Goal scorers:** Pippa ×2, Mia ×1, Taylor ×1

**Summary:** A surprisingly tough game against a much-improved Sapphires side. The Rubies spent most of the match either level or holding a one-goal lead, only for Sapphires to respond and draw level. First half was probably not the team's strongest — several positional changes trialled and some players looked uncomfortable in unfamiliar roles. Second half was better.

**Player Notes:**

**Pippa** — One of her strongest games of the season. Scored both goals while playing striker. Looked dangerous going forward. Two well-earned goals.

**Mia** — Outstanding throughout. Consistently won possession, carried the ball forward and made ground when others were struggling to progress. Scored one goal and was one of the best players on the park.

**Taylor** — Difficult first half, sitting too deep. Responded strongly after half-time, pushing higher, becoming more involved in attack and converting a goal.

**Kiera** — Solid in defence as always. Reliability and composure at the back.

**Poppy & Nixie** — Strong defensive games, worked hard throughout.

**Sienna** — Performed very well in goal, making several important saves. One of Sapphires' goals came from a corner that rolled through the box — area to address.

**Sydney** — Grew into the game and became more effective as the match progressed.

**Charlotte** — Looked uncomfortable playing on the wing, frequently drifted back into midfield.

**Naomi** — Showed plenty of energy but needs to improve positional discipline; tends to roam.

**Simone** — Difficult afternoon in defence, found it challenging to deal with some attacking pressure. Continued to work hard and never stopped trying.

**Key theme:** Players kicking the ball forward and then stopping rather than following their pass and supporting the next phase. "Pass and move" — this is the biggest development opportunity from this game.

### Game 9 – vs Springfield United | Win 6–3
*Date: 20 June 2026*

**Goal scorers:** Sydney ×2, Pippa ×1, Taylor ×1, Ruby ×1, Own Goal ×1

**Summary:** One of the most impressive performances of the season, defeating previously undefeated Springfield United. The team remained composed against Springfield's physicality and attacking threat throughout. Despite conceding three goals, the defensive unit worked tirelessly and repeatedly recovered. The six goals against one of the competition's strongest teams was one of the best attacking returns of the season.

**Players of the Match:** Simone, Kiera, Ruby

**Opposition overview (scout notes):**
- Springfield entered undefeated — one of the strongest opponents faced this season.
- **Primary attacking strategy:** Goalkeeper → defender → long deep kick to **#7 or #13** who have **exceptional pace** and run in behind defences.
- Majority of dangerous moments originated from this long-ball pattern.
- Springfield also played a **highly physical style** and competed aggressively throughout.
- **Recommendation:** Track runners, prioritise recovery runs, defensive communication to manage the pace threat.

**Player Notes:**

**Sydney** — Outstanding attacking performance. Scored two goals, remained a constant threat. Used pace well and made intelligent attacking runs.

**Pippa** — Worked hard up front and contributed an important goal. Continued to pressure defenders and create opportunities. Next step: making decisions a touch earlier when crossing — several occasions where two or three players were in the box but the delivery came late.

**Taylor** — Scored a goal and provided strong attacking support. Continued to improve movement and positioning in advanced areas.

**Ruby** — Outstanding contribution at both ends of the field. Defended strongly throughout and scored a goal. Excellent determination and commitment from start to finish.

**Kiera** — One of her strongest defensive performances of the season. Read the game well, tracked runners consistently, made several important defensive interventions. Particularly effective recovering to cover Springfield's pace out wide.

**Simone** — Played on the left wing despite it not being her preferred position. Worked tirelessly, contributing both defensively and offensively. Her effort and willingness to play for the team had significant impact on the result.

**Development areas:**
- Managing pace in behind: tracking runners, recovery runs, defensive communication
- Defensive shape against wide players: showing attackers to the touchline, covering runners at the far post, supporting defenders in 1v1 situations

### Game 10 – vs Bardon Bandits | Win 3–2

**Goal scorers:** Taylor, Elsa, Pippa

**Note:** This game featured guest/reserve players — **Elsa, Emily, Jaeger** (not in regular Khula squad). Not available: Poppy, Mia, Sienna.

**Summary:** Another well-earned 3–2 victory. For much of the game the Rubies were in control, building a 3–0 lead through disciplined defending, strong runs from wide areas and excellent link-up play. The final few minutes became frantic — after losing some defensive structure, the Bandits scored twice in quick succession. A reminder that every game lasts until the final whistle. Although Rubies scored three goals, could easily have been more — six or seven excellent opportunities where dangerous balls were delivered across the face of goal without being converted.

**Player Notes:**

**Taylor ⭐** — Strong performance, especially first half. Several excellent runs down the wing, covered plenty of ground, deservedly opened the scoring. Delivered a number of dangerous balls across the goal.

**Charlotte ⭐** — Standout from the regular squad. Excellent work rate, constantly involved around the ball, kept making herself available. Energetic and influential game.

**Kiera ⭐** — Outstanding in defence again. Positioning and ability to get between the attacker and goal remains exceptional. The timing of the opposition's two late goals — shortly after she rotated off — illustrates her defensive impact.

**Ruby** — Terrific team-first performance. Volunteered to play GK in the first half, made several good saves and distributed exactly as coaches wanted (releasing quickly down the wings). A complete performance.

**Naomi** — Handled second-half GK duties well, produced one outstanding diving save. Two late goals knocked her confidence a little, but they came during a period of general defensive instability.

**Sydney** — Dependable performance through midfield. Linked play well, made strong runs, kept the team moving forward.

**Pippa** — Capped her game with a well-earned goal. Continues to find dangerous attacking positions. Next step: making decisions a touch earlier — several occasions where two or three players were in the box but the delivery came late.

**Simone** — A quieter game than usual. Didn't have as much influence but continued to work hard.

**Nixie** — An off day by her usual high standards. Looked less engaged than normal. Every player has those days — she'll bounce back.

**Elsa (guest)** — Excellent contribution. Scored the second goal, played well on the wing and in midfield, made strong runs and consistently delivered dangerous crosses into the box.

**Jaeger (guest)** — Very impressive. A powerful runner who used her strength well, protected the ball effectively and showed good technical ability.

**Emily (guest)** — Settled into the defensive unit well and quietly did everything asked of her. Solid and reliable.

**Team focus:**
- Attack the back post and six-yard box when crosses are delivered
- Maintain defensive concentration until the final whistle
- Continue using the width of the field
